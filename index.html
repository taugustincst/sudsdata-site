Harm-reduction and SUD program work, recorded once and reported everywhere it's owed.

SUDS runs the day-to-day of California harm-reduction programs, SUD providers and the counties that fund them: street outreach, named or anonymous and offline where you allow it; naloxone and supplies by site and lot; referrals; 42 CFR Part 2 consent; CalOMS Tx; and opioid-settlement reporting. It works with or without an EHR, and beside one (SmartCare included) without replacing it.

Harm reduction

For syringe services, naloxone distribution and street outreach

Most harm-reduction programs run on spreadsheets, REDCap and monthly forms. SUDS keeps the same work in one place, built for a van, a site and a phone.

OUTREACH

One tap from the street

Log a contact, a call or a text from a phone with one + Log button. Contacts can be named or anonymous (an SSP participant code still counts), and an offline copy syncs with the office when the program turns it on.

SUPPLIES

Naloxone and supplies by site and lot

Naloxone by product, fentanyl and xylazine test strips, syringes by size and sharps containers, kept by site and lot with expiry dates. Each encounter draws down the van or site it came from; expiring lots are flagged.

REPORTING

Reports to the people who fund you

A naloxone distribution log, an SSP summary, overdose reversals, a funder report, and settlement spending by allowable use. Each follows the published fields as SUDS understands them; confirm them against your funder's current template before you submit.

COUNTY FUNDERS

A settlement roll-up for the county

A program sends its county a signed file of the outcomes and spending for the settlement funds that county pays for: contacts, kits, test strips, reversals, people served and linked, by fund. The county sees every funded program in one view, without client records.

What it does

Built for SUD programs, not adapted to them

General EHRs treat substance-use treatment as a note type. SUDS treats it as the whole job — because for a county SUD system, it is.

42 CFR PART 2

Consent & segmentation, enforced

Part 2 records are segmented by consent, not by policy memo. A disclosure is checked against the consent on file, for its purpose and its recipient, and recorded for the accounting of disclosures (ready for when that right takes effect). Every access is written to a tamper-evident audit chain.

CALOMS

CalOMS admission & discharge

CalOMS Tx admission and discharge records, with code sets checked field by field against the DHCS Data Dictionary v3.0. The submission file layout is confirmed with your county and DHCS during the pilot, before anything is submitted.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Settlement & grant accountability

Track services against the funding that paid for them. SUDS ties clients, services, and dollars together so settlement and grant reporting is an export, not a reconstruction.

OUTREACH

Outreach & referral tracking

Street outreach encounters, referrals out, referrals in, and what actually happened to them — the follow-through counties are asked about and spreadsheets can't answer.

OPERATIONS

Program operations

Caseloads, tasks, notes, and supervision workflows for navigators, clinicians, and supervisors — role-based views matched to how SUD programs are actually staffed.

REPORTING

Reports a director can use

Program, provider, and county-level reporting generated from live program data — census, services, outcomes, and the audit trail behind every number.

Security & compliance

Stated plainly, and verifiable

What SUDS does, and what is not in place yet. The evidence is public: the security questionnaire (pre-answered, with the file or test behind each answer), each control's state today, the accessibility conformance report and the software bill of materials on each release.

  • Encryption. Client details are encrypted in the database (AES-256-GCM), and backups are encrypted before they are written. SUDS Server's compliance check reports whether the host's own disk is encrypted.
  • Backups checked, not assumed. Scheduled backups are copied to a second location you choose and compared by size and SHA-256; a failed or empty copy is reported, never silent, and SUDS warns when the "offsite" folder is on the same disk.
  • Recovery drills. A drill restores a backup on its own and checks integrity, the audit chain and sign-in, with a signed report; it can run every month. A drill on the vendor's own server, from its backup copy, passed on 11 October 2026.
  • Access control. Role-based permissions, multi-factor authentication, automatic lockout, and a tamper-evident audit log whose anchors are kept outside the database.
  • Agreements. AugustInnovations LLC offers a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement and a standalone Qualified Service Organization Agreement under 42 CFR 2.12(c)(4). The templates are available for your counsel's review before anything is signed.
  • Scope. SUDS is purpose-built for SUD program operations and reporting. It is not a general EHR and does not replace one: no billing, prescribing, or medication administration.
