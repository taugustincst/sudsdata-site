What it does

Built for SUD programs, not adapted to them

General EHRs treat substance-use treatment as a note type. SUDS treats it as the whole job — because for a county SUD system, it is.

42 CFR PART 2

Consent & segmentation, enforced

Part 2 records are segmented by consent, not by policy memo. Disclosures follow the consent on file, redisclosure limits travel with the record, and every access is written to a tamper-evident audit chain anchored outside the database.

CALOMS

CalOMS admission & discharge

CalOMS treatment records captured at intake and discharge, with code sets verified field-by-field against the DHCS Data Dictionary v3.0. Submission-layout confirmation with DHCS is a standard step of pilot onboarding, done with your county — not after go-live.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Settlement & grant accountability

Track services against the funding that paid for them. SUDS ties clients, services, and dollars together so settlement and grant reporting is an export, not a reconstruction.

OUTREACH

Outreach & referral tracking

Street outreach encounters, referrals out, referrals in, and what actually happened to them — the follow-through counties are asked about and spreadsheets can't answer.

OPERATIONS

Program operations

Caseloads, tasks, notes, and supervision workflows for navigators, clinicians, and supervisors — role-based views matched to how SUD programs are actually staffed.

REPORTING

Reports a director can use

Program, provider, and county-level reporting generated from live program data — census, services, outcomes, and the audit trail behind every number.

Security & compliance

Stated plainly, and verifiable

Every claim below describes the production system as it runs today. Ask us to demonstrate any of them during a pilot — the evidence is generated by the system itself.

  • Encryption at rest. The production data volume is encrypted with LUKS (AES-256, XTS). Backups are encrypted before they leave the host.
  • Backups that are proven, not assumed. Every scheduled backup is copied off-host and verified against the original by size and SHA-256 after every run. A failed copy is reported, never silent.
  • Recovery drills. Backups are regularly restored from the off-host copy and checked end-to-end — integrity, audit chain, and sign-in — producing a signed drill report.
  • Access control. Role-based permissions, multi-factor authentication, automatic lockout, and a tamper-evident audit log whose anchors are stored off the database.
  • Agreements. AugustInnovations LLC signs a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement and a standalone Qualified Service Organization Agreement under 42 CFR 2.12(c)(4) — the instrument that actually authorizes receiving Part 2 records. Hosting is on AWS, under an accepted AWS BAA.
  • Honest scope. SUDS is a SUD-specific layer, not a general EHR, and it does not replace one. It runs the current release, 1.25.6.
